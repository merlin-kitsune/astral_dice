## 1.3.0

### New Content

#### Signs & Skills
- **Target selection now uses click-to-pick controls: left click confirms, right click reports that a skill cannot be self-cast, right click + sneak cancels**: with a target selected - **left click = confirm** (if the crosshair is not on a legal target you only get a "no valid target" message and nothing is submitted); **right click = use on yourself**: by default no skill in this mod can be self-cast, so you only get a one-line "this skill cannot be used on yourself" message - the selection is **not** submitted and the session stays open so you can keep aiming at something else (**exception: the Game Master sign's (ren) active allows self-casting - right click uses it on yourself, and the guidance line adds that option**); **right click + sneak = cancel**; **Esc** still opens the vanilla pause menu, which cancels the selection as soon as it opens; **J** (the active skill key) still cancels at any time. At the same time the **Enter confirm key** and the old behaviour of "swallowing keyboard input while selecting" are gone - movement, F3, the inventory, chat and other mods' keys all work normally while selecting; only the left/right mouse buttons and the scroll wheel are taken over (⚠️ the **"hold to select" sessions of effect cards do not take over the scroll wheel** - scrolling keeps switching slots, see this version's "Bug Fixes / Signs & Skills"), and the command chat window still works (the automated tests rely on that channel too).
- **The target selection box is redrawn as a translucent prism that hugs the creature's actual visible shape**: previously the border was drawn from the collision box alone, so a zombie's outstretched arms, a spider's eight legs or a horse's head and neck stuck out of it; it is now **collision box ∪ the model shape measured every frame**, plus half the line width of margin ⇒ on the sides and on top the border **can only be slightly larger than the creature, never smaller** (model animation and baby scaling follow along); the **bottom face is additionally lifted by 0.02 block** so the lower edge does not sit flush with the ground and flicker (it used to sink into the top face of the ground block). Colours and widths are unchanged: green for friendly / red for hostile / yellow for neutral, with the thickest line (1/16 block) on the target under the crosshair, a thinner one (1/24) when the crosshair is on something you cannot select, and the thinnest (**1/128** - halved again on 2026-09-19 to set it further apart from "aimed at") for other selectable targets in range; **the crosshair only has to land inside a target's outline to count as aiming at it**: the hit test and the outline use the **very same box** (collision box ∪ the model shape measured every frame, grown by half the line width), so a zombie's outstretched arms, a spider's legs or a horse's head and neck - visible but outside the collision box - can be aimed at too, and the crosshair no longer has to sit precisely on the collision box; it is still **rendered locally only** - other players cannot see your selection box in multiplayer.

- **New epic sign "Game Master" (ren)**: Passive "Mouse Rescue" — if the wearer has gone 5:00 without a Mouse Shield, they gain 1 random card plus the Mouse Shield (it always applies to the wearer). Active "Brat's Privilege" — use the target selector to pick any player (or press right mouse to use it on yourself) and grant the target player 1 random card plus the Mouse Shield (**the target selector shows this skill as "Mouse Shield"; the sign tooltip and the handbook still call the active "Brat's Privilege"**). The Mouse Shield = 5 yellow hearts (10 absorption) + Resistance + 1 Counterattack charge: the moment the yellow hearts are used up the shield vanishes immediately (together with the Resistance and any unused counterattack charge), while the shield itself has no time limit (the countdown in the effect bar is only a refresh window); an attacked shield wearer spends 1 charge to inject one counterattack hit into the attacker (the same counterattack formula as the Gunsmith's flaw and Pandaman's taunt). **The effect bar shows only two icons, "Mouse Shield" and "Counterattack"** — the Resistance that comes with the shield hides its icon (it still applies as a plain number), and the counterattack charge gained an icon of its own. The recipe is the basic-dice tier (4 paper + 3 star coins), and the sign is included in the creative tab, the `astral_dice:signs` summary tag, the accessory slot tag, the bounty reward pool and the handbook.
- **New third-person visible look for the Mouse Shield**: a shielded player is wrapped in a **complete pale-blue semi-transparent energy sphere** (centred on the body, 1.2 blocks in radius, so the player is fully enclosed) that **other players can see as well** (visibility is broadcast by a dedicated shield-state sync packet - vanilla does not sync mob effects to anyone but the entity itself and its passengers; see Bug Fixes); the surface pattern keeps flowing, with an added glow shell and an upward energy band, so it reads as flowing energy rather than a still glass dome; in first person your own sphere is not drawn, so the shell cannot fill the screen.

Active "**Baize's Blessing**" — a target-selector skill: choose a player **within 16 blocks** or **use it on yourself**; the target gains "Baize's Blessing", and while it lasts every heal that would go beyond full health is **converted into attack power in equal amount** (a heal that merely reaches full health produces no overflow, and the bonus never stays behind); the effect lasts until **the next Dice Blessing ends** — if the target had **no** Dice Blessing when it was applied ⇒ it vanishes as soon as that Dice Blessing ends; if the target **already had** one ⇒ **the current one is skipped** and it vanishes after the **next** Dice Blessing ends. The caster also gains **1 Blessing Talisman** and converts **every Misfortune Talisman they hold** into the same number of Blessing Talismans. The recipe is diamond dice + golden star plate (legendary tier), and the sign is included in the creative tab, the sign summary tag, the accessory slot tag and the handbook (with a page of its own).

Passive "**Fox Light**" — a stack resource (cap **20**, **kept through death**): you (and your Descent target) gain **+1** stack per **attack card crafted, obtained or equipped**, and casting Descent immediately grants **+3** stacks; Fox Light base = your attack power before casting + 50% of the target's attack power (locked at cast). **Anti-farming**: attack cards **picked up from the ground grant no stacks** (drop one and pick it back up and you gain nothing), and inserting the same combat card into the dice card slots over and over **grants no stacks either** (deduplicated by the highest number of attack cards ever equipped at once — only genuinely holding or crafting more cards keeps raising stacks). The caster's death or logout **does not affect** the Descent on its target, and the caster's bonus only goes away when the Descent ends; it is removed immediately when the target dies or logs out. The recipe is diamond dice + golden star plate (legendary tier), and the sign is included in the creative tab, the sign summary tag, the accessory slot tag and the handbook.
- **New rare sign "Oasis Queen" (nardis)**: Passive "**Intimidation**" — every **attack card equipped** in the dice grants **+2 attack power** and every **defense card equipped** grants **+2 defense power** (computed on the fly, so unequipping the sign or the cards removes the bonus at once; temporary cards count as well). Active "**Queen's Privilege**" — immediately gain **2 random combat cards and 1 random effect card** (**exclusive cards are never given**) plus the **3:00** "Queen's Privilege" effect (the HUD shows a timer using the sign's icon); **temporary cards can only be equipped or used**: they carry an enchantment glint, **cannot be dropped** and **cannot be put into chests, ender chests, shulker boxes or any other container** (they may only stay in your inventory, your hands and the dice card slots); when the timer runs out (or the effect is removed, the player dies, or it expires while offline and the player logs back in) **every temporary card is removed at once, including the ones already equipped in the dice**. **The skill can be cast again while the effect is running** (limited only by its cooldown, which starts the moment you cast): the new temporary cards **stack** on top of the existing ones (**nothing is cleared**) and the timer is **reset to 3:00**; **you can hold at most 9 temporary cards** (cards already equipped in the dice count too), and casting is blocked once you are at the limit. **Casting is blocked while your inventory has fewer than 2 free slots** (the cooldown is not consumed and a "not enough inventory space" message is shown). The effect only ends when the **3:00 timer runs out**, the effect is removed or the player dies - **using up every card no longer ends it early**. Cards are granted through the mod's shared card-granting funnel, so "card gained" interactions (Vitamin Pill healing points, the Cult Leader sign's Fox Light stacks) still trigger. The recipe is a golden dice (no star plate, rare tier), and the sign is included in the creative tab, the sign summary tag, the accessory slot tag, the handbook and the **bounty reward pool**.
- **New legendary sign "Mamushi" (mamushi)**: Passive "**King of the Marsh**" - whenever the wearer **makes other characters gain cards**, the wearer gains "**Awakening**" stacks counted **per beneficiary** (one stack per other character, **at most 3 per single handout**; cards handed to yourself do not count). At **8 stacks** the wearer enters "**True Dragon Form**": **+5 attack power**, the active skill **loses its range limit** (everyone in the same dimension), and every "**Bite**" in the hands, inventory and dice card slots **turns into a "Dragon's Roar"** (the active skill hands out a Dragon's Roar from then on); **Awakening and True Dragon Form survive death and only reset when the sign is unequipped** (the effect bar shows True Dragon Form with the sign's icon). Active "**Chain Reaction**" - you immediately gain **1 "Bite"**, and **every friendly teammate within 12 blocks** gains **1 random card** (**a target that currently holds no cards at all gets 1 extra card**; **with no team the skill instead targets every other player in the dimension**, still limited by range and by the player cap; **at most 32 players**, handed out **nearest first**; True Dragon Form removes the 12-block limit). **The passive also triggers on "You Have I Have" and on any future card that hands cards out - not only on this active skill.** Its cooldown is a **forced 1:00 that charge, the Current Core or anything else cannot shorten**. Recipe: **diamond dice + golden star plate** (legendary tier; materials = 4× sea lantern, gold block, blank sign, 2× golden star plate, diamond dice); it is included in the creative tab, the sign summary tag, the accessory slot tag and the handbook (with its crafting page).
- **New epic sign "Sherry" (sherry)**: Passive "**Detective's Strike**" - each attack on a **hostile target with at least 20 max health** grants **1 Reasoning Time stack** (max **5**); each stack grants **+1 attack power** and **-1 damage taken**; **1 stack is lost after every Dice Blessing ends**. Passive "**Friend's Guard**" - while a **teammate wears the Puppeteer Sign**, that teammate takes **1 less damage**. Active "**Strong-arm Throw**" - hurl **every hostile target within 12 blocks** to the front, then deal **2 damage** to the thrown targets and apply **1 Mark stack**; at **5 Reasoning Time stacks** the thrown targets take **5 extra damage**. **Reasoning Time survives death and only resets when the sign is unequipped** (the effect bar shows it with the sign's icon and stack count). Recipe: **diamond dice + golden star plate** (epic tier); it is included in the creative tab, the sign summary tag, the accessory slot tag, the **bounty reward pool** and the handbook (with its crafting page).

Passive "**Friend's Blessing**" - **passing a player wearing the Sherry Sign** grants them **Strength II (1:00)** + **Resistance (1:00)** and **1 Reasoning Time stack**; **this ability triggers only once per 1:00**. Active "**Float Magic**" - grants you **Witch's Float (1:00)**: during it your **movement speed is +20%**, **fall damage is -100%**, **every melee attack on you is dodged**, and **you cannot use an ender pearl to teleport**. - **Fixed the Mouse Shield's third-person look not rendering at all from other players' view** (reported in-game on 2026-09-23): the render condition used `entity.hasEffect(REN_SHIELD)`, but **vanilla never syncs mob effects to anyone but the entity itself and its own passengers** - the whole jar only constructs `ClientboundUpdateMobEffectPacket` in 4 places (`ServerPlayer`'s effect add/update, `PlayerList.sendActiveEffects`, `LivingEntity.sendEffectToPassengers`), all of them addressed at the entity itself or its passengers, and `ServerEntity` contains no effect-sync code at all. Vanilla's two dedicated "visible to others" channels (the glow outline via `setSharedFlag(6)` and the effect particles via `DATA_EFFECT_PARTICLES`) both go through `SynchedEntityData`, which carries **only the appearance, never the effect instance**. So on every other client that check was always false and the sphere was visible only to its own wearer (in third person). It is now driven by a **dedicated shield-state sync packet**: whenever a shield is granted, cleared or removed by an outside path, the server broadcasts the **full list** of currently shielded players to everyone, and sends one more full list to each player on login (`entityId` is only unique within a single server session, so the full-list replace doubles as the cross-server stale-id cleanup).

#### Items, Chips & Trading
- **New exclusive effect card "Blessing Talisman"** (exclusive to the Fengshui Master Sign): holding the card in your **main hand** opens the target selector automatically (**no countdown** — the selector closes as soon as the card leaves your main hand, and nothing is consumed before you confirm); you may choose **any player within 16 blocks (any team)** or **use it on yourself**; once confirmed the target recovers **2** health and the **current card round's play count goes up by 1** (**+1 per card**, stacking with the Ninja Sign's one-off +1 and still bound by the global cap of **9 plays per round** — using it right at the cap exactly cancels its own cost, so no cooldown starts). The card is **exclusive**: it binds to its obtainer when granted, **only the obtainer can use it**, and in anybody else's hand it neither opens the selector nor gets consumed.
- **New rare exclusive effect card "Misfortune Talisman"** (exclusive to the Fengshui Master Sign): holding the card in your **main hand** opens the target selector, and it can **only be used on hostile targets** (hostile mobs, angered neutral mobs, and **players who are not on your team**; **teammate players, passive mobs and yourself cannot be selected**); once confirmed the target takes **1** damage. Holding cost, "**Misfortune**": the **Misfortune stacks in the effect bar equal the Misfortune Talismans you currently hold** (when the count reaches 0 both the effect and its periodic damage disappear), and **every 2:00** you take damage equal to the cards held **at that settlement moment** — that 2:00 timer starts **once**, when you first hold a card, and afterwards **only settlement advances it**: the count moving between 1 and 5 **never restarts it**, so picking cards up or putting them away neither lengthens nor shortens the cycle. You may drop it freely and store it in any container, including shulker boxes and bundles (**stored in a container** it no longer counts toward Misfortune or its periodic damage). This card is **exclusive** as well: only the obtainer can use it.
- **New exclusive combat card "Bite"** (Mamushi Sign only): cost **2**, durability **1**, attack power **+3**. Once equipped, **triggering the Dice Blessing** grants **1 "Awakening" stack** and **+current Awakening stacks (up to 4) attack power** until that Dice Blessing ends (stack changes during the blessing apply **live**; each equipped copy triggers separately), so equipping it at **7 stacks** and triggering the blessing **immediately enters True Dragon Form**. Like exclusive effect cards it **can only be used by the player it was granted to**, has no recipe and never appears in any random or bounty pool.
- **New exclusive combat card "Dragon's Roar"** (Mamushi Sign only): cost **3**, durability **5**, attack power **+3**; a hit target gets **Slowness III (1:00)** and **loses 4 defence (1:00)** (repeated hits only refresh the timer, they do not stack). It can only be obtained by a Mamushi Sign wearer in **True Dragon Form**: on entering that form, every "Bite" in your hands, inventory and **already equipped in the dice** **immediately turns into a "Dragon's Roar"** (inside the dice card slots, the copies whose higher cost no longer fits are **discarded**); it is likewise an **exclusive card** usable only by the player it was granted to.

- **New "Shooting Star" chips: "Purple Shooting Star" (epic) and "Golden Shooting Star" (legendary)**: **passing by a hostile target without attacking it** deals **1** damage (**2** for the golden one) to it and grants you **1 Starlight**; the golden chip additionally deals damage equal to **your current Starlight** when the target is an **elite or a boss**. It triggers **once every 10 seconds**, and the two chips **share one timer**. The damage is true damage (ignores armour and armour toughness) and the kill is credited to the player. The particle falls from **high above the target's head** and **counts as a hit the moment it touches the top of that head**, where the damage is applied; it falls **1.2× faster than before** (the distance grows by that same 1.2× while the **total fall time stays 1 second**, so it starts from higher up); when both chips are equipped the **purple particle falls first**, and the golden one follows **1 second after the hit**. The timer **creates no effect** — its remaining time is shown in the item tooltip only. Recipes follow the **Starlight school** (purple: 3 amethyst shards + 2 star coin dust + blank chip + 3 star plates; golden: 3 glowstone dust + 2 star coin dust + blank chip + 3 golden star plates), and they are available in the creative tab, the chip summary tag, the accessory slot tag, the **bounty reward pool** (purple only; the golden chip is legendary and stays out of every pool) and the handbook (with crafting pages).
- **New "Star Coin Wallet"**: star coins and star coin bags can now be **deposited into a wallet** whose balance lives on the player and is **unaffected by death drops**. A **wallet button** appears at the **top-left** of the inventory screen — one click converts **every** star coin and star coin bag in the 36 inventory slots and the offhand into balance (**1 star coin = 1, 1 star coin bag = 9**). Two **withdraw** buttons (star coin / star coin bag) sit next to it: **left click withdraws 1**, **Shift + left click withdraws as many as possible** (limited by both the balance and the free inventory space; nothing is deducted when nothing can be withdrawn). **Newly obtained star coins go straight into the wallet by default** (both the grant path and the ground-pickup path; star coins exchanged by the merchant sign also land in the wallet), while **anything that spends star coins from the inventory is unchanged** (the star coin hammer and the like still only see physical star coins and bags in the inventory and offhand). The interface consists of **one balance bar plus three buttons**: the bar is a **96x24 dark rounded panel** that shows the current balance **right-aligned at its far end** (the server reconciles the value once per second and syncs it **only when it actually changed**, so the bar keeps up within a second no matter whether the change came from a button, a picked-up coin, the merchant sign's exchange, or a third-party mod writing to the ledger); each button has its **own hover highlight texture** (the wallet button lights up with a warm golden glow, the two withdraw buttons turn blue-framed) and the withdraw buttons carry a **vanilla-style item-slot border**; the interface artwork (the balance bar and all three button textures) is supplied by the player and is **byte-identical across both loaders**. Control positions and sizes are pixel-aligned and **differ between the survival and creative screens** (in creative they appear only on the Inventory tab). The balance ledger lives in the front-end library `starengine_lib` (a new `economy` package with a public API for other mods); debug command `/starcoin add|set|remove|get|rank`. Seven new config options: `enable_star_coin_wallet` (default `true`), `deposit_star_coin_on_obtain` (default `true`, **only effective while the wallet is enabled**) plus x/y offsets for the wallet button and the two withdraw buttons. An **incompatibility list** was added as well: when a third-party currency mod that fully duplicates this wallet is present the game treats it as incompatible and **refuses to start** (halting on the mod-loading error screen), telling the player to remove it, since running two systems that book the same star coins is unsafe. **Amended 2026-09-22 - the check is now conditional on the wallet**: it only refuses to start while the **Star Coin Wallet** is enabled (`enable_star_coin_wallet = true`, the default). Setting it to `false` means this mod no longer keeps a player-level currency ledger, so coexistence is safe and the game **starts normally** (no check runs, no error is shown); the error message was rewritten accordingly to offer both ways out - (1) turn `[star_coin_wallet] enable_star_coin_wallet` off to coexist, or (2) keep the wallet and remove that mod. Because the decision now requires the config, the check moved from the **mod construction stage** to **common setup** (configs are only loaded after construction, so they cannot be read from the constructor); the path that gets the message onto the screen is unchanged. **Fixed 2026-09-22**: (1) **the wallet bar no longer vanishes when you switch to creative mode** - the "show only on the Inventory tab" rule used to be applied to *every* control, but the creative screen always reopens on the default "Building Blocks" tab, so the whole bar was judged hidden; now **the balance bar and the wallet button show on every creative tab**, and only the two withdraw buttons stay limited to the Inventory tab (they sit on top of the vanilla item grid and would block slots on other tabs). (2) A **widget visibility self-lock** was fixed at the same time: the old code marked a widget invisible while hiding it, so it **never came back even after switching to the Inventory tab - the screen had to be closed and reopened** (which is exactly why reopening the creative inventory was the only way to see it). (3) **The balance bar now keeps up within the same tick**: balance changes caused by *this mod itself* (deposit/withdraw buttons, picked-up star coins) are pushed to the client **immediately** instead of waiting for the next once-per-second reconciliation, which remains as a fallback covering third-party mods and the `/starcoin` command.

#### Commands & Debugging

- **New `/astralparty` subcommand `finishsigncooldown [targets]` (finish the active-skill cooldown immediately)**: writes the player-level cooldown attachment `sign_active_cooldown_end` to the **current game time**, i.e. "the cooldown just finished this instant" (the same technique the Current Core chip uses), so an admin can retry a sign's active skill right away. It **only ends the cooldown timer**: it leaves the locked ("in effect") state `sign_active_lock_*`, the cooldown baseline `sign_active_max_cooldown` and the Mamushi sign's forced-cooldown hard gate untouched - so neither "the skill is still in effect" nor "we are inside the forced-cooldown window" is affected (in the former case there is no running cooldown to end anyway: the cooldown starts only after the in-effect window ends). Permission level (OP level 2), target argument (optional `@a`/player name, executor by default) and everything else match the other subcommands, and it takes **no numeric argument**; use `/astralparty dump` afterwards to check the `SIGN` group's `sign_active_cooldown_end` and `sign_active_lock_*`.

### Content & Balance

#### Damage & Resolution
- **The Living Page is now a "hostile-target selector + high-speed flying strike" card instead of a passive spell-damage buffer**: it can only be used on **hostile targets** (hostile mobs ∪ angered neutral mobs; never players, never yourself) - holding the card in your **main hand** opens the target selector automatically (no countdown; putting the card away closes it and nothing is consumed before you confirm), with a **32-block lock-on range that includes vertical distance** (a target straight above you, or down in a pit, is selectable just the same - the range is no longer horizontal-only). The lightning's **damage type is now true damage** (`astral_dice:true_damage`, registered in `minecraft:bypasses_armor` → **ignores armor and armor toughness**; Protection and Resistance still apply): vanilla `minecraft:lightning_bolt` is not in that tag (verified against the game's own resources), so a new `EntityThunderHitMixin` swaps the damage source **only for lightning spawned by this chip** (marker set `RailgunBolts`, registered in `RailgunChipItem#strike`), the **hit scope is now restricted to hostile mobs and angered neutral mobs (e.g. an enderman after being attacked)** - the attacker, friendly pets, neutral animals, armour stands and the like are dropped entirely (no damage, no ignition, whereas vanilla lightning hits them all); every other vanilla lightning behaviour (ignition, charged creepers, zombified-piglin/witch conversion, the `onEntityStruckByLightning` event) is untouched; death-message text for the new type was added for both languages and both versions. **Where the scope is enforced (added 2026-09-14 after independent verification)**: the whitelist was moved **from `Entity#thunderHit` up to `LightningBolt#tick`'s target selection** (new `mixin/LightningBoltStrikeScopeMixin`, an `@ModifyArg` that ANDs the whitelist into the original `Entity::isAlive` predicate; the injection point was verified instruction-by-instruction with `javap -c` against both merged jars). The reason: turtles / villagers / pigs / mushroom cows **override `thunderHit` without calling `super`**, so the old implementation - which only cancelled inside `Entity#thunderHit` - never affected them (turtles were still one-shot by `Float.MAX_VALUE`, villagers still turned into witches, pigs into zombified piglins, mushroom cows still changed skin), and neither the `onEntityStruckByLightning` event nor the `CHANNELED_LIGHTNING` ("Very Very Frightening") advancement were bound by the entity whitelist (the advancement receives the same entity list). With the check moved up, non-hostile targets are **removed before the loop is even entered**: no damage, no conversion, no event, no advancement; block ignition, lightning rods and the other world behaviour stay vanilla. Mobs **owned by the lightning's caster** are excluded as well (`OwnableEntity#getOwnerUUID()` == `bolt.getCause().getUUID()`), so an angered tamed pet (e.g. your own wolf) is no longer struck by your own lightning. **The scope's hostility test was aligned with the global two-argument rule (2026-09-15)**: it now uses the context overload `HostileTargets.isHostile(viewer, target)` with `viewer` = the player who called the lightning down (`bolt.getCause()`, written by `RailgunChipItem#strike`), so **players you treat as hostile** (not on your team and having actively attacked the caster) are struck by the railgun lightning as well, exactly like every other area/splash effect (under the old single-argument test players never counted); the degraded overload without a lightning instance has no source player and still leaves players out.
- **Damage effect cards (spell damage) now deal true damage**: the card bonus on a ranged/magic hit is no longer merged into the same damage event (where it was reduced together with the weapon damage by armour, armour toughness and Protection) but is settled separately through `ModDamageTypes.trueDamage(level, player)` - **only the card part ignores armour**, the base weapon damage is unchanged. The area damage of Directional Blast and the Electric Glove was switched to true damage as well.
- **Unified the "hostile target" rule**: a single entry point `combat/HostileTargets.isHostile(Entity)` was added, defined as **hostile mobs (`net.minecraft.world.entity.monster.Enemy`) ∪ angered neutral mobs (`NeutralMob#isAngry()`, i.e. `getRemainingPersistentAngerTime() > 0`)**; all **24 gameplay checks per version** now go through it (the Piercing Gun gate, the area filters of Directional Blast / Electric Glove / the Boss sign splash / Target Chip / Hand Fans, the Investigation Stage bonus, the taunt counterattack, the trigger and kill counters of Cursed Sword / Electric Sword / Smart Watch / Satellite / Flashlight / Adrenaline / Railgun, Lulu's slow, the Hacker's stealth break and passive type, and Bonnie's Key Clue). Previously only the railgun lightning performed the anger check, so **angered wolves / iron golems / polar bears / bees** (vanilla has exactly 6 direct `NeutralMob` implementers and only these 4 are not `Enemy`) were missed everywhere else, while endermen / zombified piglins / piglins are `Enemy` themselves and therefore always did count - and still do **even when calm** (this ruling keeps that behaviour). Five historical checks that bypassed the entry point with the **fully-qualified** `net.minecraft.world.entity.monster.Enemy` were corrected as well (Directional Blast AOE, Target Chip nearest target, the Boss splash, the Hacker's stealth break, Bonnie's Key Clue). The **loot injection pool** (`instanceof Monster` in `LootInjectionHandler`, 0.3% star plate from monster-class mobs) is a drop-pool rule rather than a hostile-target rule and was deliberately left alone.
- **Mark stack cap raised from 16 to 32**: the maximum number of marks (`marked`) is now the **fixed constant** `GameplayConstants.MAX_MARKER = 32` (it used to be the `max_marker` config option, now removed - see the next entry); the single entry point that applies a mark, `MarkManager.apply`, still clamps the effect level to `MAX_MARKER - 1`, so the cap is **32 layers** (the number comes from the vanilla label `enchantment.level.N` and is shown on the **inventory effects-panel label and its hover tooltip only for 2-10 layers (amplifier 1..9)** - at 11 layers and above no number is displayed; the vanilla HUD draws icons only) (identical on both versions).
- **True damage is no longer reduced or swallowed by invulnerability frames**: a new data-pack tag `data/minecraft/tags/damage_type/bypasses_cooldown.json` lists `astral_dice:true_damage` (**neither the 1.21.1 nor the 1.20.1 client-extra resource jar ships this tag file, so this mod's file is its definition**; every entry of both jars' `data/minecraft/tags/damage_type/` directories was checked), so the `invulnerableTime > 10` early-out in `LivingEntity#hurt` no longer applies to true damage (the same check in both versions: 1.21.1 `LivingEntity.java:1190` / 1.20.1 `:1131`). Beneficiaries: the effect-card spell-damage bonus, the Directional Blast / Electric Glove area damage and the Boss sign splash - all of them settle as nested `hurt(true_damage)` and fell exactly inside the 20-tick window vanilla had just written, so the whole amount was dropped whenever `bonus <= lastHurt` (measured: an arrow for 1 plus a laser for +4 landed only 3). **Side effect stated plainly**: the railgun lightning uses the **same** `astral_dice:true_damage` type and therefore pierces invulnerability frames too; `astral_dice:dice_damage` (counterattack injection and the Crimson Die / King Power self-damage) is **not** on the tag and is unchanged.
- **Hostile-player rule (global, user ruling 2026-09-15)**: a player who is **not on your team and has actively attacked you** is always treated as a hostile target (the record lives in the new `combat/PlayerHostilityTracker`, a server-side in-memory static table), so **the Boss sign splash and the railgun lightning (aligned 2026-09-15 with `viewer` = the lightning's source player `bolt.getCause()`; see the Railgun entry) now hit such players with the same effect**; `HostileTargets` gained a context overload `isHostile(Entity viewer, Entity target)` (the single-argument overload keeps its old semantics - players never count), and the Boss splash, the Investigation Stage bonus and the Piercing Gun gate now use it. The record **excludes** this mod's internal area/splash settlement (`DiceCombatEvents.isInternalAoe()`) and counterattack injection (`isInCounterChain()`), and never records self-damage or a non-player on either side; the stance is cleared when a player **dies** (LOWEST, after the saving effects have decided whether to cancel the death), on the death clone and on **logging out** (the records where the player is the attacker and where they are the victim are both dropped; nothing is persisted, so a server restart clears the table).

#### Signs & Skills
- **Boss sign splash moved to the passive and made single-shot**: when a Dice Blessing is triggered while Recharged Energy is at 5 layers, **consume 2** (replacing the usual -1, and those 2 layers are only spent when the splash **actually lands**) and, **immediately after this attack's damage is finalized**, deal **88%** of that damage (**minimum 5**) to hostiles **within 6 blocks of the target (the target included)**. The damage type is now **true damage** (`astral_dice:true_damage` → **ignores armor and armor toughness**; Protection and Resistance still apply) and it still comes with explosion particles and sound (**visuals only**), **breaks no blocks** and hits **no friendlies** (hostile targets only). The **splash never reached the main target** and that is fixed: vanilla `LivingEntity#hurt` writes `lastHurt`/invulnerability from the **pre-dice-combat weapon damage** before the mod's damage event fires, so the splash fell into the "lower damage inside the invulnerability window is dropped" branch and vanished (measured: the main target lost only the 3 melee damage while 5 splash damage disappeared); the main target's invulnerability is now cleared for that one hit, and it takes melee + splash in full again. The old implementation ("active consumes 2 → keeps cleaving every attack, 6 blocks, other targets, for the next Blessing") is gone, and the active skill no longer spends layers.
- **Play-round wind-down rule (user ruling, 2026-09-15)**: a round that did not reach the cap **also enters one 30-second cooldown once all of its effect timers have finished** - the test is "play count below the cap and `EffectCardPeriod.getRemainingBlockTicks(player) <= 0`" (the remaining lock time is derived automatically from the registered "effect pending" sources, with no hard-coded effect list), and the duration reuses the same `ChargeManager.cooldownTicks` basis as hitting the cap. It does **not** void the remaining plays: this branch does not top the count up to the cap, so `isBurstFull` stays false and cards remain playable during the cooldown, with the count cleared when the cooldown expires. The "hit the cap -> enter the cooldown" invariant is unchanged; a cooldown **already running** must not be restarted or extended by cards played during it (otherwise playing later would stretch the cooldown and a continuously playing cycle would never end). The **death path no longer calls `EffectCardPeriod.clearRoundBonuses`** either (none of those keys is `copyOnDeath`, so a fresh entity starts at the defaults), leaving `registerPlay`'s round boundary, `tick`'s round end and the Ninja's grace-period force reset `forceResetRound` as the **only three** call sites - from 2026-09-15 all three share one and the same cleanup list: besides the one-shot play bonus / Candy chip / Satellite chip / living-page cycle accumulator, **the Electric Glove's spell-damage spread armed for the current round (attachment `electric_glove_aoe`) is disarmed inside `clearRoundBonuses` as well** (`ElectricGloveChipItem.disarmAoe`, re-armable in the next round; the call used to live only in `tick` case 1, so the Ninja's grace-period force reset did not disarm it and the two "round fully reset" paths had inconsistent cleanup).
- **Sign active skills are now a three-state machine: ready → locked (in effect) → cooldown → ready**: previously an active skill had only "ready / cooldown", so any active that applies a timed effect started its cooldown the moment it was pressed, the cooldown finished while the effect was still running, and it could be triggered again immediately. It is now split into three states - when a trigger actually **applies a timed effect / its own timer**, the skill enters the **locked (in effect)** state: it may only enter the active-skill cooldown after **every timer this skill applied has finished** (all expiry ticks computed at trigger time taken as `max`, which is the gate's hard upper bound), and while locked **key presses do nothing** and show "%s: active skill is still in effect!" (new key `msg.astral_dice.sign_active_in_effect`); leaving the lock **always starts the cooldown, with no gap**. The test is `BaseSignItem.isSignActiveLocked` (hard upper bound not reached **and** this sign's gate effect instance still present) and the transition out is `endLockAndStartCooldown`. Actives that apply no timed effect and the three targeting standby skills (Haiqing / Bonnie / Moses) behave exactly as before (they still start the cooldown on trigger/release).
- **The 7 signs that lock, and the 9 that do not**: **locking (7)** - Parunan, Jasmine, Misaki, Fanny (**only on the branches that actually apply a timed effect**; a branch that only hands out items does not lock and starts the cooldown directly), Papara, Nancy Lu and Fen; their actives apply a self-side timed effect (or write a self-side expiry tick), so they enter the locked state on trigger. **Not locking (9)**, with reasons: Mimi / Rin / Padman **only hand out cards** (they produce no timer at all); Lulu / Pandaman apply **target-side effects only** (a slow or taunt on a hostile mob does not mean the caster's own skill is still in effect); Haiqing / Bonnie / Moses have **only the 30-second standby window**, which is ruled **not** to count as this skill's timer (the standby is a "waiting for you to pick a target" window, not a skill-effect duration, and the existing design where a miss on timeout costs no cooldown must be preserved, so they never enter the locked state); and Komachi has **no timer whatsoever**, so the dedicated rule in the entry below takes over.
- **Reductions used while locked now accumulate into a pool instead of changing the current cooldown**: a locked active **does not write** `sign_active_cooldown_end`, so "Fate Guidance", "Express Delivery" (Jasmine's passive) and Lulu's -10 s had nothing to modify while it lasted. They now **accumulate into a pool** (attachment `sign_active_reduction_pool`) during the lock and are **deducted in one go when the cooldown really starts** afterwards - **actual cooldown = the actual baseline at cooldown start - the pool** (`effective = max(0, base - pool)`); the pool is then zeroed and `sign_active_max_cooldown` is rewritten to `effective`. The baseline is still written into `sign_active_max_cooldown` **when the lock begins**, so the key every reducer reads and its arithmetic are unchanged (minimal diff). Reductions used outside the locked state behave exactly as before (they still modify the cooldown end directly).
- **Ninja-only rule: the lock follows the effect-card cycle, plus a 1:00 grace backstop**: the Ninja's active "Ninjutsu Combo" applies no timer at all (it only adds +1 to the current play round's allowance), so it **does not enter the cooldown immediately** - instead the **lock follows the effect-card cycle**, and **the cooldown starts at the moment that round's play state fully resets** (`EffectCardPeriod.onRoundFullyReset` → `BaseSignItem.onEffectCardRoundReset`; the reset points are only the two boundaries, `tick` case 1 and `registerPlay`'s boundary block). A **1:00 grace window** (counted from the trigger) additionally applies: if **no effect card at all was played** during it, the play state is **force-reset and the active skill enters its cooldown** (`EffectCardPeriod.forceResetRound` plus the cooldown); once any card has been played, the grace backstop is void and the play cycle is followed instead (the evidence is written to attachment `sign_active_lock_played` in `registerPlay` and **survives the cycle boundary** - `effect_card_play_count` / `effect_card_bonus_plays` cannot be read because both are zeroed at that boundary).
- **Current Core chip: strictly disabled while locked**: while the active skill is locked (in effect), the chip's "spend charge to finish the cooldown instantly" **cannot be triggered and spends no charge**, and it throws the **same** message as the three-state change, "%s: active skill is still in effect!" - the key press is already intercepted by the locked branch of `BaseSignItem.performSkill` (that branch is tested before the cooldown branch), and `CurrentCoreChipItem.tryFinishCooldown` repeats the same test as defence in depth; the chip can finish the cooldown instantly for charge **only in the real cooldown state**, and the skill still has to be pressed again afterwards (existing behaviour unchanged).

### Bug Fixes

#### Damage & Resolution
- Fixed the **Ender Die's "damage taken in rain/underwater +40%" being dropped entirely on the dice-combat path**: the old code changed the amount directly in `LivingDamageEvent.Pre` (1.21.1) / `LivingHurtEvent` (1.20.1), but dice combat **overwrites** the event with its own final damage via `setNewDamage`/`setAmount`, so the +40% vanished whenever a blessed player was hit in melee. It is now a **victim-side damage modifier** registered in `DiceCombatModifiers` (a live predicate with no stored state - unequipping the die or leaving the rain/water drops it immediately); the `EnderDiceHandler` HIGH listener is the **single application point**, evaluating it once and recording the multiplier for that damage instance, and the dice-combat path only **carries that same multiplier over** instead of consuming it a second time - so **one damage event applies it exactly once (x1.4)**. The pre-existing cross-version difference in *when* it lands (after armor on 1.21.1, before armor on 1.20.1) is **unchanged**. The **1.20.1 double consumption (x1.96) is fixed**: on that version the curse ratio is captured in `LivingHurtEvent@LOWEST`, i.e. **after** the victim-modifier application point (HIGH), so the captured ratio already includes this x1.4 and is applied once more by the dice-combat built-in factor - carrying the multiplier over as well would make x1.96, so it is skipped when `dice_curse_ratio > 1`; 1.21.1 captures in `LivingIncomingDamageEvent` (before the application point), where the ratio carries no such term, so no guard is needed there.
- Fixed the **Electric Glove's 3-block AOE splash using a different damage baseline per version (1.20.1 was higher)**: both versions take "the value of the current damage event" as the AOE baseline inside `SpellDamageRegistry`'s `onHit`, but the **event timing differs** - 1.21.1's `DamageEffectCardHandler` hooks `LivingDamageEvent.Pre` (**after armour and enchantment reduction**), while 1.20.1 hooked `LivingHurtEvent` (**before armour**, `LivingEntity.java:1665`), so 1.20.1 splashed the **pre-armour raw value** and hit harder around armoured targets. Following the damage-event mapping already recorded in `AGENTS.md` (`LivingDamageEvent.Pre` <-> 1.20.1 `LivingDamageEvent`, i.e. after armour), 1.20.1's spell-damage main chain now hooks `LivingDamageEvent` and the type of `combat/SpellDamageContext.event` was synchronised (the reader in `SpellDamageRegistry` now takes the post-armour value from `ctx.event.getAmount()`, matching 1.21.1's `getNewDamage()`); `AGENTS.md` now states explicitly that this chain must **not** be hooked back onto `LivingHurtEvent`. Residual (inherent to the platforms, also recorded in `AGENTS.md`): the baselines still differ when the target has **absorption** hearts (1.20.1 dispatches after absorption, 1.21.1 before), which ordinary mobs never have, so the practical impact is negligible.
- Fixed **1.20.1's damage numbers being practically invisible (a third of 1.21.1's lifetime)**: a floating number's lifetime is advanced once per client tick, but on 1.20.1 it was advanced **three times** within the same client tick - `client/ClientTickHandler` had no phase guard (Forge's `TickEvent.ClientTickEvent` fires START *and* END every tick) and advanced it twice, while the END callback in `client/KeyBindingSetup` advanced it once more; the same `DURATION = 40` therefore lasted only about **13 ticks (0.67 s)**, with the alpha fading to zero across that same window, which players see as "the damage number flashes once and is gone / is never visible at all". That leaves only a ~0.67 s window - an **independent** defect that would be too short even with correct drawing; the **direct cause** of 1.20.1's "damage numbers never appear at all" is the projection defect in the next entry. 1.21.1's counterpart is `ClientTickEvent.Post` (exactly once per tick, i.e. 2 s), so **only 1.20.1 was affected**. The advance now has a **single call site** (`ClientTickHandler`, `TickEvent.Phase.END` only) and the duplicate call in `KeyBindingSetup` is gone. **Measured in game (1.20.1: sword in hand, die equipped, melee attack on a surviving zombie)**: server `send id=... type=minecraft:zombie` -> client `add ... hasEntity=true` -> overlay `draw ... x=432 y=169 alpha=242` (the draw path really runs, and the geometry cull reports `reason=behind_camera` exactly as expected) -> the entry lives **1963-1970 ms over 40 tick calls** (= exactly one per client tick), matching 1.21.1's 40 ticks (2 s).
- Fixed **1.20.1's damage numbers never being displayed (targets straight ahead were discarded as "behind the camera")**: a floating number's screen position is computed client-side with a **hand-rolled world-to-screen projection**, and the 1.20.1 port had **copied 1.21.1's view-matrix formula** `new Matrix4f().rotation(camera.rotation().conjugate())`. The two loaders' vanilla view rotations are **not equivalent**: 1.21.1's `GameRenderer#renderLevel` really does use exactly that expression (`neoforge` source lines 1272-1273), while 1.20.1 builds it as `mulPose(Axis.XP.rotationDegrees(camera.getXRot()))` + `mulPose(Axis.YP.rotationDegrees(camera.getYRot() + 180F))` (`forge` source lines 1127-1128), which differs by a **180-degree Y rotation** and a **flipped pitch sign** (1.20.1's `camera.rotation()` is `rotationYXZ(-yRot, xRot, 0)`, whose inverse is not that expression). The copied formula therefore produced a **negative `w`** for a target straight ahead, so the `w <= 0` "behind the camera" branch dropped it on every single frame and the numbers were **never** drawn (1.21.1 is unaffected because its formula matches its own vanilla renderer). The 1.20.1 view matrix is now built exactly like its own vanilla renderer, while the 1.21.1 code is **left unchanged** and annotated so nobody "syncs" it into a regression. **Measured in the user's production pack (auto-spawned zombie 3 blocks straight ahead + a real melee hit with a diamond sword, 5 rounds)**: server `send` 5 -> client `recv` 5 -> `add` 5 -> survives **40 ticks (2 s, matching 1.21.1)** -> the overlay logged `skip reason=behind_camera w=-3.0` **1064 times and `draw` 0 times** (a target 3 blocks ahead always measured `w=-3.0`), matching the derivation exactly. **Re-verified in game after the fix (dev 1.20.1, same auto-trigger: a zombie spawned 3 blocks straight ahead plus real melee hits with a diamond sword over 5 rounds)**: `send` 5 / `recv` 5 / `add` 5, and the overlay logged **`draw` 1042 times with `behind_camera` 0 times** (previously 0 draws / 1064 behind-camera skips), drawing at `x=240` (exactly `guiW/2`, i.e. the target dead ahead) with `y` floating from 159 up to 144, alpha fading 248 -> 121 and a lifetime of **40 ticks (2 s)**, exactly matching 1.21.1. Residuals (inherent to the platform): the view matrix omits the `ComputeCameraAngles` roll (that value only exists on the event object and is not stored on the camera; normally 0), and the FOV uses the raw `options.fov()` value instead of the effective value computed by vanilla's private `getFov(..., true)` (a small offset while sprinting).
- Fixed **the missing Dice Blessing HUD icon (it was only visible in the inventory)**: the blessing used to be applied with the **five-argument `MobEffectInstance` constructor** `(effect, duration, amplifier, ambient, visible)`, which is equivalent to `showIcon = visible` at the bytecode level (both loaders' merged jars disassemble to `iload 5; iload 5` into the six-argument `(...ZZZ)V` constructor) - so passing `visible=false` (only meant to suppress the potion particles) also **hid the HUD icon**, and since the inventory effect panel does not read `showIcon`, the blessing remained visible there alone and players got no on-HUD hint of how long it had left. It now uses the **six-argument constructor** `(..., false, false, true)`, the same "particles off, icon kept" convention as this mod's other status effects (Charge, Empower, Weakness Reveal, Healing, Investigation stage), so the blessing icon and its countdown appear again in the HUD effect bar. - Added the long-standing "Version Gate" rule (see AGENTS.md): a client and a server may only interconnect when the **minor (major.minor) part of their `mod_version` matches** — `1.2.x` ↔ `1.2.y` is allowed while `1.1.x` ↔ `1.2.0` is rejected; the criterion is **derived automatically from the project version and never hard-coded**, so raising the minor version (1.2→1.3) needs no code changes. Derivation chain: `gradle.properties`' `mod_version` → (at build time `generateModMetadata` expands the template `src/main/templates/astral_dice_version.properties`) → the resource `astral_dice_version.properties` (shipped inside the jar) → (at runtime `network/VersionGate.majorMinor()`) → the interop number (`1.2.0+neoforge_1.21.1`/`1.2.0+forge_1.20.1` → `1.2`; `1.1.3` → `1.1`). Each loader uses its own native mechanism: (1) `neoforge-1.21.1` passes the interop number as the payload registrar's network version (`RegisterPayloadHandlersEvent#registrar(String)`; NeoForge compares it per channel with `String.equals` during the **configuration phase**, and on mismatch sends `ModdedNetworkSetupFailedPayload` and disconnects); (2) `forge-1.20.1` passes it as the `SimpleChannel` protocol version (both `clientAcceptedVersions`/`serverAcceptedVersions` use `VersionGate::accepts`; FML's **login handshake** exchanges channel versions and both sides validate them, disconnecting on mismatch) and replaces Forge's default `MATCH_VERSION` display test with the same criterion via `IExtensionPoint.DisplayTest` (`ModLoadingContext#registerDisplayTest`) — the default demands the **full version string** to be identical, which is stricter than this rule and would mislabel same-minor pairs such as `1.2.0` ↔ `1.2.1` as incompatible. What the player sees when rejected: on 1.20.1 Forge's "mod versions do not match" screen (mod name plus the two full versions under "You have / Server has" and the instruction to install the same version as the server); on 1.21.1 NeoForge's "Connection Lost" mod-version-mismatch screen (channel name plus the failure reason, which contains both interop numbers). Single-player and dev runs are unaffected (the integrated server and the client come from the same build and therefore share the interop number) (both loaders).
- **Added an optional patch for an upstream NeoForge 21.1.x bug (it cannot cause a startup failure)**: NeoForge 21.1.235's `LivingEntity#hurt` `return false`s on the "damage event cancelled" branch **without popping the `DamageContainer`**, so every cancellation leaves one container on the stack forever (all three of this mod's cancellation points hit that path: the Hacker's pearl-fall immunity, the Gunsmith's weakness dodge and Adrenaline-High's dodge); the upstream fix (popping in the cancel branch) has **not been backported to any 21.x**. A separate mixin config `astral_dice.neoforge_fixes.mixins.json` (**`required: false` + `injectors.defaultRequire: 0` + injectors `require = 0`**) injects HEAD/RETURN into `hurt` and **restores the entry stack depth** instead of blindly popping — after a future upstream backport the patch degrades to 0 pops and therefore **never double-pops, never throws `EmptyStackException` and never fails startup with a `MixinApplyError`** (all three future scenarios verified against the runtime Mixin and FML sources); the first time it acts it writes one `[astral_dice/neoforge_fixes] …` liveness line to `logs/latest.log`, so its absence later tells you the patch silently stopped working (the cost of `required: false`). Semantics are covered by a pure-Java test (`tools/run_mixin_stack_sanitizer_test.ps1`, 10/10). ⚠️ **Fixed (verified in-game, 2026-09-13)**: the patch's helper classes `NeoForgeFixesLog`/`DamageStackSanitizer` originally sat inside the declared mixin package `...mixin.fixes`, and Mixin **refuses to load non-mixin classes from that package** (`IllegalClassLoadError: ... is in a defined mixin package`), which crashed the server/client on the **very first real `hurt`** (observed path: `Player#attack` → `Zombie#hurt` → `LivingEntity#hurt` → the injected `recordDamageContainerDepth` → class load → crash, `crash-reports/crash-*-server.txt`). Both helpers now live in the ordinary package `com.merlinkitsune.astral_dice.fixes`, leaving only the real `@Mixin` (`LivingEntityDamageContainerMixin`) in the mixin package; the pure-Java test still passes 10/10. **NeoForge 1.21.1 side only**: 1.20.1 has no `DamageContainer` damage pipeline (platform difference).
