# Astral Dice 1.3.3 Changelog

Released: 1.3.3 — a **balance and bug-fix** release with no new items. Four main things: (1) four "on being hit" effects can no longer be farmed with environmental or self-inflicted damage; (2) the skill rules of the Fengshui Master, Cult Leader and Secret Detective Signs were reworked; (3) the action bar's broken colouring and missing text are fixed; (4) a batch of wording and handbook corrections.

Note! The prerequisite mod StarEngine Lib is bundled inside this mod (embedded 1.0.5) — you no longer need to install it separately. If your mods folder still contains a manually downloaded starengine_lib-*.jar, delete it: the old file would override the embedded one.
Note! On 1.20.1 the Mixin runtime can be **Mixin Booster ≥0.1.3** **or** **Sinytra Connector** — pick either one (since 1.3.2-hotfix; previously Mixin Booster was mandatory).

## Balance & Quality Updates

- **Fengshui Master Sign "Perfect Helper" now fills Recharged Energy to the maximum**: applying Baize's Blessing to a player wearing the Boss Sign no longer grants just +1 stack — it **tops that player's Recharged Energy straight up to the 5-stack cap**.
- **Cult Leader Sign "Divine Descent": the Fox Light bonus becomes a "Pursuit" extra damage instance**: when the player carrying Divine Descent attacks a hostile target it triggers "Pursuit", and that attack **additionally deals** (the caster's own attack-power snapshot + the current Fox Light stacks) as damage.
  - This bonus **no longer counts towards Attack Power** and is settled separately after the hit lands — so it is **no longer reduced by the target's defense** and no longer participates in "Full Power" and similar multipliers. It used to be added to Attack Power; this also removes the risk that merely opening the card screen spent a Fox Light stack.
  - **Consumption is unchanged**: 1 Fox Light stack is still spent only when attacking a **new** target, later attacks on the same target keep triggering Pursuit without spending another stack, and attacking a new target with 0 stacks adds nothing.
- **Secret Detective Sign, "Investigation Stage": the old "Invisibility" is replaced by a new "Concealment" effect**: while it is active **mobs cannot target you** (the check looks only at this effect, no longer at vanilla invisibility); the moment you deal effective damage to a **mob** (melee / ranged / magic — any kind of attack counts) **Concealment is removed immediately**, and your **dice battles then add damage equal to the target's Mark layers**, **until the Investigation bonus ends**. All four stage durations are **unified at 1:00** (previously 0:15 / 0:20 / 0:30 for stages I / II / III). The new effect reuses the vanilla invisibility icon.
- **"Fate's Guide" gains a new ability: your combat dice roll is always 6 while it is active**: same convention as the Padman Sign's "next roll is 6" — **with no dice equipped it is still 0**. The tooltip ability list was revised accordingly (the `1./2./3./4.` numbering becomes dashes, with the new entry inserted after the second one).
- **Baize's Blessing / Divine Descent now start their timer on any landed attack**: the 2:00 countdown used to require a **melee** weapon attack; it now starts on **any attack — melee or ranged** (arrows / projectiles / spells, i.e. any damage caused by you) **as long as it actually lands** (the damage really is applied), and the target must still be a legal Dice Blessing target. The timer still **starts only once and never resets**.
- **Slime Sign: area healing removed from the active skill**: the whole "centred on them, apply Instant Health to players / pets / rideables within 16 blocks" clause was deleted. The area part now only applies **Slowness (1:00)** to hostiles; the main effect (the target gains +3 Healing + Instant Health) is unchanged.
- **Big Bowl of Stew: friendly-creature healing removed, healing points raised to 2**: it **no longer heals friendly creatures** when the blessing ends; healing points go from **1 to 2**, and the wording now explicitly says **"you and all friendly players within 16 blocks"**.
- **Text clean-ups**: the duplicated "it is dodged by the Gunsmith; dodging triggers an automatic counterattack that includes Weakness Insight layers" in the Gunsmith Sign's "Broken" active and passive descriptions now reads **"it is dodged and triggers a counterattack"** (mechanics unchanged); "Unwavering"'s description now simply reads **"defense +4"** instead of "armor +8 per stack (equal to +4 defense in dice battles)" (text only — the underlying attribute is unchanged); the Charge card dropped its redundant "must be placed in the card slot before the blessing" parenthetical.

## Bug Fixes

- **Fixed the action bar's broken colouring and missing text**:
  - **Missing text**: the two success messages of the Secret Detective Sign's "Covert Operation" and the Astrologer Sign's "Weak Mark" referenced language keys that were **missing on all three version lines**, so confirming a target showed a raw key instead of a sentence. They are now supplied in Chinese, English and Japanese.
  - **Broken colouring**: 11 action bar messages carried their own colour codes while the sender coloured the whole line yellow, producing a visible **"yellow front half, grey back half"** seam (Hanna / Fengshui / Sherry / Cult Leader / Mamushi / Mouse Rescue and others). All embedded colour codes were removed; the whole line now takes its colour from the outer style.
  - **Inconsistent palette**: following the rule that **refusal / blocking messages must be red**, 11 such messages that were still yellow are now **red** (the three Ninja Sign "not cast" notices, Oasis Queen's temporary-card cap / not enough free slots, Business Sign with nothing to exchange, Sherry Sign blocked ground / no target, Hanna Sign ender pearl blocked, Cult Leader descent already in effect, Mamushi Sign forced cooldown). "Current Core" changed from **aqua** to red when charging is insufficient and yellow when the cooldown is finished; the Mamushi forced-cooldown colour also differed per line and is now red everywhere.
  - **Two parallel channels merged**: the airbag trigger, the wallet deposit / withdraw feedback, the card inventory's "you need a die equipped" and the client-side "out of plays this round" were drawn through the **vanilla overlay** (white, at a different position from every other message) while roughly 60 other messages went through this mod's own action bar — the same piece of UI rendered by two different systems. All four now go through the mod's action bar and are coloured (yellow for the airbag / wallet, red for "need a die equipped" / "out of plays").
- **Four "on being hit" effects can no longer be triggered by environmental or self-inflicted damage; they now require a hostile attack**:
  All four keyed off "the player took **any** damage", so fall / cactus / fire / drowning / starvation and other **environmental damage**, plus this mod's own "Misfortune Talisman" self-inflicted true damage every 2:00 (its damage source has **no entity** and counts as nobody's attack) and any self-damage, all triggered them — a player could farm the rewards without ever meeting an enemy (Star Coins have no cap, so it was farmable):
  - the **Buffer Shield** (healing-school chip): +2 Healing / +3 Star Coins on being hit;
  - the **Mouse Shield**'s counter: *any* creature's attack consumed its single counter charge — including a teammate player's, a tamed pet's or an unangered neutral mob's — and the counter was then dealt back to the ally;
  - the **Slime Sign**'s "Cell Division": +1 Healing layer and −10s active-skill cooldown on being hit;
  - the **Vampire Sign**'s "Drain" (the taking-damage clause): heals half of the damage taken.
  All four now require **a hostile attack**, on three conditions that must all hold: there is an **actual attacker** (ruling out environmental damage and entity-less true damage), the attacker is not you, and the attacker is a **hostile target** for you (hostile mobs, neutral mobs except tamed pets, angerable tameable animals, and non-teammate players who have actively attacked you). The last three effects' descriptions and their handbook entries were updated to match.
  NOTE: the **Hanna Sign**'s "Witch's Float" is **not** part of this — its text already says "*any* melee attack on you is dodged", which was confirmed to be intended, so it is unchanged.
